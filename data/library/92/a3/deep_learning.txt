
Deep Learning Fundamentals

Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that uses artificial neural networks
with multiple layers. Geoffrey Hinton, Yann LeCun, and Yoshua Bengio pioneered
modern deep learning techniques. The ImageNet competition showed that Convolutional
Neural Networks could dramatically outperform other methods on image classification.

Backpropagation Algorithm

The backpropagation algorithm, developed by Rumelhart, Hinton, and Williams,
computes the gradient of the loss function with respect to the weights of the network.
This allows Stochastic Gradient Descent to efficiently train deep networks.

Applications of Deep Learning

Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, and Speech Recognition have all been
transformed by deep learning. OpenAI and DeepMind have produced remarkable systems.
