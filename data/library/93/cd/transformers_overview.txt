
Transformer Architecture Overview

The transformer model, introduced by Vaswani et al. in 2017, revolutionised
natural language processing. Unlike recurrent networks, transformers use
self-attention mechanisms to process all tokens in parallel, dramatically
reducing training time.

Key components include multi-head attention, position-wise feed-forward
networks, and positional encodings. The encoder processes the input sequence
while the decoder generates the output sequence token by token.

BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers) by Google
demonstrated that pre-training on large corpora followed by task-specific
fine-tuning achieves state-of-the-art results across many NLP benchmarks.

GPT-4, developed by OpenAI, extends the transformer decoder architecture to
hundreds of billions of parameters, enabling few-shot and zero-shot learning
across diverse tasks including code generation, reasoning, and creative writing.
