The History of Computing

A computer is an electronic device that processes data according to a set of instructions.
Charles Babbage is considered the father of computing for his work on the Analytical Engine.
Ada Lovelace was the first programmer, writing algorithms for the Analytical Engine.
Alan Turing is a pioneer of theoretical computer science and artificial intelligence.
The transistor is a semiconductor device used to amplify and switch electronic signals.
Integrated circuits revolutionized electronics by combining multiple transistors on a chip.
The microprocessor is a complete processor on a single integrated circuit chip.

Operating Systems

An operating system is software that manages hardware and software resources.
Unix is a family of multitasking, multiuser computer operating systems.
Linux is a free and open-source Unix-like operating system kernel.
Windows is a group of graphical operating system families developed by Microsoft.
